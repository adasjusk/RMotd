# Grant for Horizon's reviewers

`horizon/` is a Horizon port of this project, written by Vakarux12 and released under CC-BY-SA-4.0, the original's
licence. CC-BY-SA-4.0 already lets anyone read, copy and adapt the original. As the author of the port, Vakarux12
grants the reviewers of the Horizon mod registry (horizon-rust/horizon-mods) permission to read, build and check
`horizon/` in order to review it, for every version submitted to the registry.
